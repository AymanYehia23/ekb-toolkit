# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be decent. Assume good faith. Disagree about the work rather than about the
person.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep feedback focused on the work and its evidence.
- Protect other contributors' privacy. Do not post personal career data,
  private repository contents, credentials, or other sensitive information.
- Follow the project's contribution and security guidance.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, sharing
private information without permission, and any other conduct that would make
this project unsafe or inaccessible are not welcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or edit contributions, close discussions, or restrict
participation when necessary to keep this a constructive place to contribute.
Reports should be made privately to the maintainers through the contact method
listed in the repository. Please do not include sensitive information in a
public issue.

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever an individual is
representing the project in public.
