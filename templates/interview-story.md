## Story 1 — <a title you would actually say out loud>

**Feature context.** What the complete feature or engineering change did, in
two or three sentences. Describe the whole thing, not only your part; your part
comes next.

**Likely motivation.** User-confirmed context when it exists. Otherwise the
curated inference, explicitly labelled:

> *Provisional inference:* the change lands in a hotfix branch and ships the
> same day, which suggests a support issue rather than a planned refactor. Not
> confirmed.

**Participation.** `implemented`. Direct implementation wording is supported.
This does not imply nobody else contributed.

**Decision and action.** The architecture, implementation, integration, or
investigation, grounded in the record. This is the part an interviewer will
push on, so it carries the most detail.

**Result.** The delivered technical behavior, or a real measured outcome. Not a
business metric, and not an expected benefit restated as an achieved one.

**Trade-offs and limits.** Only material boundaries. What this approach cost,
and what it does not cover.
