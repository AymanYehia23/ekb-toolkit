# Controlled use of whole-candidate capabilities

Follow [screening](../prompts/screen.md) for each new opportunity. Proceed
only with explicit authorization for that frozen posting. Keep formal
qualifications separate from the skills match. A declined or stale decision
blocks matching and rendering.

Use [whole-candidate discovery](../prompts/capability-discovery.md) to find
relevant knowledge and experience across the workspace. Record confirmed
capabilities through the [profile procedure](../prompts/profile.md).
Declarations preserve provenance, proficiency, context, and interview
readiness. Project achievements, ownership, results and employment facts
still require their own evidence. Private transfer hypotheses, learning
goals and unresolved declarations stay out of public skills.

Review the [private editorial packet](../prompts/resume-editorial.md) against
exact wording, current records and the target. Compare the résumé with its
[interview guide](../prompts/interview.md#application-mode). Machine checks
can enforce citation and proficiency placement. A human decides whether
the claims and presentation are accurate and defensible.

Inspect the exact generated PDF and extracted text and record approval
against its generation and digest before delivery. Follow the
[update and recovery procedure](workflow.md#private-drafts-and-existing-pdfs)
to preserve the prior version through failed checks and keep historical
applications intact. Keep profiles, review packets, interview guides and
draft PDFs private.
